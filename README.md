## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computational Linguist and NLP Engineer** with a background spanning academic research and production AI systems.

My work sits at the intersection of **linguistics and machine learning**. I am especially interested in what happens when we treat language as more than just input to a model: its structure, context, variation, development, and the humans who use it.

I have worked across the NLP lifecycle, from **building and annotating corpora** to **training and evaluating models**, designing **conversational AI systems**, developing **language resources**, and creating **benchmarks for LLM evaluation**.

Currently, I'm particularly interested in the intersection of **AI, language learning, and cognition**, including computational learner modelling, educational NLP, and how language technologies can better understand and support human learning.

# 🚀 Featured Research

## 🔎 InFACT

### Benchmarking LLM Explanations Against Institutional Reasoning for Deliberation-Aware Fact-Checking

A Romanian-language benchmark designed to investigate a deceptively simple question:

> **Can an LLM reach the right verdict for the right reasons?**

InFACT evaluates **fact-checking predictions and generated explanations** against institutional reasoning, separating classification performance from explanation alignment.

**~800 fact-checks · Romanian · LLM evaluation · Explainability · Fact-checking**

## 🇷🇴 CoRoSeOf

### An Annotated Corpus of Romanian Sexist and Offensive Tweets

A large-scale Romanian language resource containing **39,245 manually annotated social-media posts**.

The project spans corpus creation, annotation methodology, quality control, inter-annotator agreement, and downstream NLP experiments.

**39,245 samples · Human annotation · Romanian · Sexism detection · Offensive language**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-CoRoSeOf-181717?logo=github)](https://github.com/DianaHoefels/CoRoSeOf)

## 🌳 UD Romanian TueCL

### Universal Dependencies for Romanian Social-Media Language

A Romanian social-media treebank derived from CoRoSeOf, providing **morphosyntactic and syntactic annotations** for non-canonical, user-generated Romanian.

**Romanian · Universal Dependencies · Syntax · Social Media**

# 👩‍🏫 Linguist Before the Model

My path into NLP started with **language itself**.

I studied **philology, linguistics, translation, and language pedagogy** before moving into computational linguistics. I later taught English and Romanian, worked as a Teaching Assistant in Computational Linguistics, and delivered training for teams working with conversational AI systems.

That background continues to shape how I approach NLP:

> **Start with the language. Understand the phenomenon. Then choose the computational method.**
